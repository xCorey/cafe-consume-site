Family business, two doors

How Café Consume came to be

Andrés Merlos started selling vintage clothes back in college, meeting people around campus to pass along pieces he'd found. That habit became Consume Vintage, which he opened in McKinley Park in late 2019 with his mother, Olga — the two of them still do "the hunt" together at flea markets and estate sales, curating pieces for the nostalgia and story behind them, not just the label.

During the pandemic, Andrés bought the building next door and spent the next few years turning it into Café Consume with his father and brother, Guillermo — building the bar, reupholstering the seats, and pouring the concrete themselves. The coffee is sourced from Guatemala and El Salvador, and the space stays open enough that you can watch the coffee and kitchen work happen while you wait.

Out back, the patio does double duty: live music some evenings, and on weekends it turns into a small vintage pop-up market.
